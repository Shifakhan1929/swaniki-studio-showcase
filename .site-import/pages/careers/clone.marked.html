Questions

Good to know.

Are these roles remote?

Yes. Every Swaniki internship is 100% remote and async, with flexible hours and no weekend work.

How many hours per day are required?

A minimum of 3 hours per day, up to 8. You choose your committed hours during the application — your stipend scales with them.

Is the internship paid?

There is a one-month probation period, followed by a 6-month paid internship, then a full-time role or Pre-Placement Offer (PPO).

Why do I need a Swapiki profile link?

Swapiki is a Swaniki audio product. Register, record at least 2 project audios, and paste your profile link — we use it to screen communication and clarity for all roles.

What happens to my data?

We collect your details, links, resume and (optional) photo solely for recruitment, with your consent under India's DPDP Act. Records are automatically deleted 90 days after submission.
